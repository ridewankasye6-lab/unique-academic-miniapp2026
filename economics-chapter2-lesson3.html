📖 Lesson 3 — Demand and Supply

❇️ Market Equilibrium Reference & Guide

🔺 Definition / ትርጓሜ

English: Market equilibrium occurs when market demand equals market supply ($Q_d = Q_s$).

አማርኛ: Market equilibrium የሚፈጠረው Demand እና Supply እኩል ሲሆን ወይም ፍላጎት እና አቅርቦት በእኩል ደረጃ ሲጣጣሙ ነው ::

🔻 Part 1: Mathematical Problem & Worked Solution (ምሳሌ)

Given Equations:
• Market Demand: Qd = 100 - 2P
• Market Supply: P = (Qs / 2) + 20

✔️ A. Calculate Equilibrium Price and Quantity

በመጀመሪያ Equilibrium ላይ ሁለቱ Demand እና Supply እኩል እንደሆኑ መረዳት ያስፈልጋል:: በመቀጠል ከ Supply equation ላይ Qs ን ስንፈልግ Qs = 2P - 40 ይመጣል::

Step-by-step Calculation:
Qs = Qd
2P - 40 = 100 - 2P
4P = 140
P = 35

Q = 100 - 2(35)
Q = 30

Result: Equilibrium Price (P*) = 35 and Equilibrium Quantity (Q*) = 30.

ስለዚህ Equilibrium ላይ Price 30 እና Quantity 40 ሳይሆን ተመጣጣኝ የሚሆነው Price 35 እና Quantity 30 ሲሆን ነው ማለት ነው ::

✔️ B. Determine Shortage or Surplus at P = 25 and P = 35

Price (P) Quantity Demanded (Qd) Quantity Supplied (Qs) Market Condition Amharic Explanation (ማብራሪያ)
P = 25 Qd = 100 - 2(25) = 50 Qs = 2(25) - 40 = 10 Shortage of 40 units
(50 - 10 = 40)		 P = 25 ሲሆን Demand (50) ከ Supply (10) ይበልጣል። ስለዚህ የአቅርቦት እጥረት (Shortage) አለ ማለት ነው።
P = 35 Qd = 100 - 2(35) = 30 Qs = 2(35) - 40 = 30 Equilibrium State
(No Surplus / Shortage)		 P = 35 ሲሆን Demand እና Supply እኩል (30) ስለሆኑ ገበያው በEquilibrium ላይ ይገኛል።

❇️ Part 2: Market Equilibrium Shifts & Scenarios

#Situation 1: Single Curve Shift (One Changes, Other Constant)

የመጀመሪያው ሁኔታ አንደኛው ሲጨምር/ሲቀንስ እና ሌላኛው ባለበት ሲቆይ በPrice እና በQuantity ላይ የሚፈጠረው ለውጥ::

Scenario Demand (D) Supply (S) Equilibrium Price (P) Equilibrium Quantity (Q) Amharic Explanation
1. Demand Increases Increases (↑) Constant Increases (↑) Increases (↑) Demand ሲጨምር Supply ባለበት ሲቆም Quantity እና Price ሁለቱም ይጨምራሉ ::
2. Demand Decreases Decreases (↓) Constant Decreases (↓) Decreases (↓) Demand ሲቀንስ Supply ባለበት ሲቆም Quantity እና Price ሁለቱም ይቀንሳሉ ::
3. Supply Increases Constant Increases (↑) Decreases (↓) Increases (↑) Supply ሲጨምር Demand ባለበት ሲቆም Quantity ይጨምራል፣ Price ይቀንሳል ::
4. Supply Decreases Constant Decreases (↓) Increases (↑) Decreases (↓) Supply ሲቀንስ Demand ባለበት ሲቆም Quantity ይቀንሳል፣ Price ይጨምራል ::

#Situation 2: Both Demand and Supply Increase

When both Demand and Supply increase, Quantity always increases (Q ↑), but the effect on Price depends on the magnitude of the shifts.

ሁለቱም Demand እና Supply ሲጨምሩ Quantity በእርግጠኝነት ይጨምራል:: ነገር ግን Price በ 3 ክስተቶች ላይ ተመርኩዞ ይወሰናል::

Scenario Condition Relative Change Price Effect (P) Quantity Effect (Q) Amharic Explanation
Scenario 1 Increase in Demand > Increase in Supply Increases (↑) Increases (↑) በበለጠ የጨመረው ከSupply ይልቅ Demand ከሆነ ዋጋው (Price) ይጨምራል::
Scenario 2 Increase in Supply > Increase in Demand Decreases (↓) Increases (↑) በበለጠ የጨመረው ከDemand ይልቅ Supply ከሆነ ዋጋው (Price) ይቀንሳል::
Scenario 3 Increase in Demand = Increase in Supply No Change (=) Increases (↑) ሁለቱም በእኩል መጠን ከጨመሩ ዋጋው (Price) ሳይቀየር ባለበት ይቆያል::

#Situation 3: Both Demand and Supply Decrease

When both Demand and Supply decrease, Quantity always decreases (Q ↓), but the effect on Price depends on the relative fall.

ሁለቱም Demand እና Supply በአንድ ጊዜ ሲቀነሱ Quantity ይቀንሳል:: ነገር ግን Price መጨመር መቀነሱ እንደ ለውጡ መጠን ይወሰናል::
