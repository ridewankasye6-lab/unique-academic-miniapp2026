🚀 Getting Started

New to Unique Academic? Follow these simple steps.

1
Create your account Open Registration and enter your personal information.
2
Complete registration Create your password and complete the required registration information.
3
Complete payment Follow the payment instructions shown during registration.
4
Wait for approval Your payment and registration will be reviewed by the administrator.
5
Start learning Once approved, log in and access your available courses.

📝 Registration & Login

To use protected course content, you need a Unique Academic account and an approved registration.

If you already have an account, use the Login option from the menu. If you are a new student, choose Register.

💳 Payment & Approval

After completing the registration information, follow the payment instructions displayed on the registration page.

⏳ Pending: Your payment is waiting for administrator verification.

✅ Approved: Your registration has been approved and course access is available.

❌ Rejected: Your registration was not approved. Contact support if you believe there is a problem.

📚 Courses, Chapters & Notes

From the home page, choose a subject and open the course.

Inside a course, select the chapter you want to study. You can then use the available notes, summaries and other learning resources.

Use the navigation buttons provided on each page to move through the learning material.

❓ Quizzes

Open a chapter quiz to practice what you have learned.

Read each question carefully, select your answer, and use the navigation controls to continue.

After completing the quiz, review your result and explanations to identify areas that need more study.

👤 Student Profile

When you are logged in, open Profile from the menu to access your student account.

Your profile helps you manage and view your account information.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Check that your email and password are correct. Make sure you have an internet connection. If the problem continues, contact support.
First, make sure you are logged into the same account used during registration. Refresh the page and try again. If access is still locked, contact support.
Check your internet connection and refresh the page. You can also close and reopen your browser. If the issue continues, contact support.
Use the password recovery option available on the login page. If you cannot recover your account, contact Unique Academic support.
Please contact support and tell us the subject, chapter and information that needs to be reviewed.
